Luncher is a lightweight and customizable home screen (launcher) for Android TV, small enough to run well on very weak devices and feature rich.

Currently, the home screen shows the time and date, and your TV apps as a grid of banners. You can:

- Reorder apps: hold OK on an app, then move it where you want it.
- Hide apps: move an app onto the hidden shelf, or hide it from the list in Luncher's settings.

Luncher asks for no permissions and has no internet access: no ads, no tracking, no accounts.

Custom app banners, wallpapers, grouping in folders and many more settings are planned with best performance in mind.

Making Luncher your home screen: on Android TV 5.1, the Home button asks which home screen to use. From Android 6 on, most devices always open their built-in launcher; Luncher then becomes the home screen once the built-in launcher is disabled or overridden. Detailed instructions depend on the device model.
